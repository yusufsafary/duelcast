Tokenomics

Tokenomics, honestly.

Short page. DuelCast has no token, no supply, no pool and no fees, and this page exists so that answer stays on the record.

No token issued

Most tokenomics pages explain a supply. This one records an absence, in the same ledger style as everything else in the almanac, so the answer is on the record and cannot be misquoted later.

Why no token

A duel on DuelCast is a measurement: two identified wallets, one hour, one public price. A token would add a market around that measurement without improving it, and a tradable record would invite farming instead of duelling. Standing here is worth having precisely because it cannot be bought.

What a win is worth

A line on your Banner, a counter that moves, and a rival who knows. Nothing else is promised, priced or implied. Anyone who tells you a DuelCast win converts into money is describing a different project, or inventing one.
