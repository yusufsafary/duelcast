Privacy

How little data a static, on-chain project needs, stated plainly.

What this site collects

Almost nothing. orspin.fun is a static site: no accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies set by us, and no analytics beacons in the pages we ship.

On-chain data

Wallet addresses, duels and Banner records are public blockchain records by design. The site reads them to display the board and the ledger; reading them here adds no new exposure.

Hosting logs

Our host may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and reliability. We do not sell or share log data, and we do not build profiles of visitors.
